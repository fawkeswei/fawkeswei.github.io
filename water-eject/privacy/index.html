Privacy Policy for Water Eject

Last updated: May 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Water Eject ("the App") handles your information. The App's core feature — playing a 165 Hz tone to vibrate water out of your phone speaker — runs entirely on-device and does not require an account.

Information Collection and Use

Water Eject does not ask for an email, sign-up, or any personal information. The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described below:

  • Google AdMob (free version only): Displays advertisements in the free version of the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, and approximate location to deliver and measure ads. The Pro version removes ads entirely. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Sentry: Collects anonymous crash reports and diagnostic information so we can fix bugs. Reports include device model, OS version, and a stack trace. They do not include the contents of any audio playback or your usage patterns.
  • RevenueCat: Processes in-app purchases and manages your Pro subscription or Lifetime entitlement. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user ID and Apple's transaction receipt; it does not receive your Apple ID, email, or payment details.
  • Analytics: The App logs anonymous, aggregated events (e.g. "eject started", "Pro purchased") to help us understand which features are used. Events do not include personally identifiable information.

Data Storage

Your Pro purchase state and app preferences are stored locally on your device. No personal data is stored on our servers. The 165 Hz tone is generated on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

The App does not request location, microphone, contacts, photos, or any other sensitive permission. It only uses the system audio output to play the eject tone.

Pro Version

Upgrading to Pro (one-time Lifetime purchase or monthly subscription) removes all advertisements and the AdMob SDK is no longer used to serve ads. Sentry crash reporting and analytics continue to operate so we can keep the app stable for paying users.

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect any information processed. All third-party SDKs listed above are subject to their own published security practices.

Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Deletion

Because the App does not maintain personal user accounts, there is no profile to delete. You can:

  • Reset advertising tracking at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all local data from your device.
  • Manage or cancel your Pro subscription via iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
